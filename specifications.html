Bifacial technology

Pahal TOPCon Bifacial Panels

Pahal's next-generation N-type TOPCon panels utilize double-glass encapsulation. This allows the rear side of the module to absorb reflected albedo light from the roof, generating up to 25% additional power over traditional single-sided modules.

  • Panel Efficiency exceeding 22.5%
  • Low light performance optimized for Kerala monsoon
  • 30-Year Linear Output Performance Warranty
Distribution & Safety

Havells Wiring & DC Distribution

The balance of system is wired using heavy-duty copper cables from Havells, Finolex, RR Kabel, or Polycab. High-voltage protection is handled through custom Havells DC distribution boxes equipped with surge protective devices to isolate the inverter from lightning strikes.

  • UV-resistant XLPE insulation class
  • Surge protection (SPD) on DC input lines
  • Flame-retardant low-smoke (FR-LSH) cabling
