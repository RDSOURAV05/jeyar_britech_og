About JEYAR BRIGHTECH

Powering homes with clean, affordable solar energy across Thiruvananthapuram.

Our Mission & Story

Established in January 2026, JEYAR BRIGHTECH Private Limited is an MNRE-registered solar enterprise serving residential and commercial properties across Thiruvananthapuram. Backed by a 15-member multi-disciplinary team of skilled engineers, technical experts, and accounts specialists, we have successfully executed close to 50 diverse projects within our first six months—ranging from 3kW residential arrays to a massive 25kW Public Works Department (PWD) venture.

Under the leadership of Mr. Santhosh Kumar J. R., who brings over 30 years of industry experience (including serving as Kerala Branch Head for General Electric and Havells), we ensure seamless end-to-end execution, handling all engineering approvals and utility paperwork for a hassle-free green transition.
